This work generates synthetic financial misinformation only to measure and improve robustness. The market and the company are fictitious. Every generated document carries a mandatory provenance label and a safety tag, and generated text never leaves the simulator: our figures and replay viewer show only labels such as edit type, never generated text. The harness has no broker connection or order-routing path, the attacker's objective is decision change rather than profit, and no component disseminates content. The LLM runs locally, so no generated text is sent to an external service. We release the framework for defensive research and discourage its use on real news feeds.
