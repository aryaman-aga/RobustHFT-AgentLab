\section{Evaluation Protocol}\label{sec:protocol}
\textbf{Paired simulation.} Every run drives two agents over the same market path (Fig.~\ref{fig:harness}). The \emph{treated} agent reads clean and injected news under the attacker's bias prompt; the \emph{shadow} agent is an identical clean twin. Both have their own portfolio, memory and defense layer, so a flip is a paired, same-day comparison. All randomness flows from seeds derived by hashing (master seed, component, day, item). The attacker is seeded by its injection type rather than by the arm, so every arm of a seed draws the same attacker random numbers: common random numbers~\cite{law2015simulation}.

\textbf{Metrics.} The misinformation exposure rate (MER) is the share of retrieved documents that are synthetic. The attack success rate is the share of evaluation days $\mathcal{T}$ with a flip,
\begin{equation}
\asr=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{T}|}\sum_{t\in\mathcal{T}}\mathbf{1}\!\left[a_t\neq a_t^{\mathrm{clean}}\right].
\end{equation}
\emph{Active} ASR counts only flips in which the treated agent trades. It discounts the defensive Holds a risk layer creates, which plain ASR scores as attack successes.

\textbf{Adaptive and frozen attacks.} In the \emph{adaptive} setting the attacker's feedback loop runs against the defended agent. In the \emph{frozen} setting the attack recorded against the undefended agent, i.e.\ its documents and daily bias prompts, is replayed verbatim against every defense. Differences between frozen arms are therefore caused by the defense alone. Replaying the tape against its own source agent must reproduce that run exactly, and the pipeline checks this on every seed.

\textbf{Placebo controls.} Injecting documents has side effects that have nothing to do with what they say: added documents reshuffle retrieval and flip some near-threshold decisions, and a defense should not be credited for removing that noise. The \emph{placebo} therefore injects a meaning-preserving paraphrase of every clean item, using synonym and connector substitutions that never touch directions, numbers, entities or causes. We report \emph{net ASR}, $\asr_{\mathrm{attack}}-\asr_{\mathrm{plc}}$, with each defense's own placebo. Matched placebos that add the attack's article count and bias prompts one at a time split ASR further into retrieval noise, bias prompts and content (Appendix~\ref{app:ladder}).

\textbf{Statistics.} The unit of replication is a seed, i.e.\ one simulated market with its own news, prices and attacker draws. All arms of a seed share the market path, so every comparison is paired by seed. We report the mean per-seed difference with a 95\% percentile-bootstrap interval ($10^4$ resamples)~\cite{efron1993bootstrap} and a two-sided sign-flip permutation $p$-value (exact for $n\le 16$, otherwise $2\times10^4$ random flips)~\cite{good2005permutation}, Holm-corrected within each family of related comparisons~\cite{holm1979simple}. We call a difference \emph{detectable} only if its interval excludes zero \emph{and} $p_{\mathrm{Holm}}<0.05$.
