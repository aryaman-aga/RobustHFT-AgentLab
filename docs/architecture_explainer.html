🔬 Base Paper vs. Our Extensions

📄 Base Paper (AutoRedTrader)

  • MisGen Engine: 3-way attack space (Bias Context + 8 Minor Perturbations + Style Rewrites).
  • Pólya-Urn Sampler: Reinforcement sampling where successful decision-flipping strategies get boosted.
  • Quality Filters: Similarity (\(A_{\text{sim}}\)) & Detectability (\(A_{\text{det}}\)) gates to maintain plausibility.
  • Attacker Focus: Maximizes Attack Success Rate (ASR) to find LLM vulnerabilities.

🚀 Our Extensions (RobustHFT-AgentLab)

  • Temporal Grounding: 30-day historical analogs with strict admissibility (\(e + H \le t\)) to prevent lookahead.
  • Microstructure Features: High-frequency proxies (Garman-Klass RV, Order Flow Imbalance, Amihud illiquidity).
  • Explicit Risk Layer: Post-policy confidence gates, Hold-on-Conflict rules, volatility sizing, drawdown throttle.
  • Methodological Rigor: Introduced Placebo Arm and Net ASR to eliminate structural retrieval noise.
