B.Tech project · offline red-teaming lab for trading agents

Can fake financial news trick a trading agent?

We slip subtle, synthetic misinformation into a simulated LLM-style trading agent's news feed, watch which decisions flip, and measure what actually protects it. Everything runs offline on synthetic data. No real news is altered, and no trade ever leaves the simulator.

How it works

One trading day, step by step

Two identical agents trade the same market in lockstep. One reads only clean news. The other also reads whatever the attacker injected. Any day they decide differently is a flip, and the share of flipped days is the attack success rate (ASR). Click any box to learn more.
